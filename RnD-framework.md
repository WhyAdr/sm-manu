### 3. Secondary Metabolite BGCs, Metabolic Capacity, and Virulence Profiling
- **3.1 Biosynthetic Gene Clusters (antiSMASH)**: antiSMASH v8.0.4 overview maps (`Figure3_SM-NMZ-antismash-overview.jpeg` / `Figure3_C14-NMZ-antismash-overview.jpeg`) showing 11 detected BGC regions per chromosome. Interpret product calls as biosynthetic potential; independent red/pink culture phenotype and LC-HRMS results support the reported prodigiosin- and serrawettin-associated observations.
- **3.2 Distillation of Microbial Metabolism (DRAM)**: Functional metabolic distillation heatmaps (`Figure3_DRAM.jpeg` / `Figure3_DRAM2.jpeg`, presented as **Figure 4A/B**) covering carbon, nitrogen, sulfur, and energy-conversion categories across 13 *Serratia* genomes and the *Prodigiosinella aquatilis* PA-LS101 outgroup. DRAM module scores represent annotation-derived genomic potential rather than measured activity or pathway completion.
- **3.3 Comparative Virulence-Factor Profiling (VFDB / VF_classifier)**: Comparative virulence-factor analysis (`Figure4_vf_heatmap.jpg`) contextualized against environmental, MEOR, and bioremediation comparative genomes. The profile describes sequence similarity and genomic repertoire; it is not a formal biosafety determination.
- **Figure 3**:
  > **[Figure 3]**: *antiSMASH v8.0.4 overview maps showing 11 predicted BGC regions on each chromosome, including NRPS/NRPS-like loci, NRP-metallophores, the prodigiosin-associated pig locus, beta-lactones, terpene precursors, RiPPs, redox cofactors, and an opine-like metallophore. The map does not by itself establish product identity or production.*
- **Figure 4**:
  > **[Figure 4]**: *DRAM module-completeness heatmaps across the 14-genome panel, covering central-carbon, respiratory, CAZy, nitrogen, sulfur, arsenate-reduction, photosynthesis-control, and methanogenesis/methanotrophy categories. Scores summarize annotation evidence rather than direct physiological activity.*
- **Figure S1 (Supplementary Figure)**:
  > **[Figure S1]**: *Comparative virulence-factor heatmap (VFDB / VF_classifier) contrasting SM-NMZ and C14-NMZ against the environmental comparative panel (moved to Supplementary Materials due to vertical length); the panel provides genomic context rather than a standalone biosafety assessment.*

- **4.2 Biosurfactant and Pour Point Depressant (PPD)-Associated Synteny**: Syntenic conservation of biosurfactant/bio-emulsifier loci interpreted alongside the red culture phenotype and LC-HRMS profiling, which detected serrawettin W1 and serratamolide C:
  - Serrawettin BGC synteny (`Figure6_serrawettin.jpeg`).
  - Prodigiosin BGC synteny (`Figure7_prodigiosin.jpeg`).
- **Figure 5**:
  > **[Figure 5]**: *HADEG subpathway bubble plot illustrating the distribution and genomic marker repertoire of aerobic hydrocarbon-biodegradation genes across the comparative genomes.*

### 5. LadA Flavin-Dependent Monooxygenase Phylogeny and Pan-Proteome Orthology
- **5.1 LadA Flavin-Dependent Monooxygenase Identification, Phylogeny, and Micro-Synteny**:
  - Maximum-likelihood phylogenetic placement tree (`Figure8_LadA-validation-phylotree.svg`) of candidate LadA-like monooxygenases ($ladA\alpha$, $ladA\beta$, $ladB$) constructed using MAFFT + ClipKIT + IQ-TREE 3 (`Q.PFAM+R3`, 1,000 UFBoot2) and compared with 19 oxidoreductase reference controls. Report the relevant branch-support values in the Results text.
  - Curated genomic-neighborhood synteny (`Figure9_LadA-cluster-curated.jpeg`) of candidate *ladA*-associated loci analyzed with `clinker`.
- **5.2 Comparative Pan-Proteome Orthology (ProteinOrtho)**:
  - Proteome-wide orthology clustering across the 14-genome dataset using ProteinOrtho (v6.3.6) with DIAMOND backend (v2.2.5).
  - Quantifying core proteome conservation, accessory genome variation, and strain-specific orthogroups (represented syntenically via the LadA cluster visualization in Figure 9).
- **Figure 8**:
  > **[Figure 8]**: *Maximum-likelihood phylogenetic tree (IQ-TREE 3, Q.PFAM+R3 model) placing candidate LadA-family flavin-dependent monooxygenases relative to reference oxidoreductases.*
- **Figure 9**:
  > **[Figure 9]**: *Curated genomic-neighborhood synteny (clinker) of candidate ladA-associated loci across SM-NMZ, C14-NMZ, and comparative genomes; conserved sequence placement does not establish biochemical activity or substrate turnover.*

> **Interpretive guardrail for Section 5:** Phylogenetic placement is sequence-level evidence. It should be accompanied by branch-support values and should not be presented as proof of biochemical activity, substrate specificity, alkane turnover, or pathway completion.
